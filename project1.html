Neural Correlates of Pranayama

Investigating cortical oscillations, autonomic state shifts, and fronto-parietal phase synchronization during controlled yogic breath regulation.

Focus Area Respiratory Neuromodulation & Autonomic Control

02 / SET UP

Experimental Configuration

64-Channel EEG Setup

High-density cap placement following the standard 10–20 system, targeting frontal and parietal leads to capture fronto-parietal coherence and slow-wave dynamics.

Biometric Sync

Simultaneous recording of chest wall excursion via piezoelectric respiratory transducers alongside continuous PPG for real-time heart-rate variability (HRV) alignment.

03 / TASK

Protocol & Behavioral Paradigm

A structured block design comparing baseline rest with paced rhythmic respiration and retention cycles.

01

Resting Baseline

5-minute continuous recording under spontaneous breathing conditions with eyes open/closed.

02

Paced Breathing Protocol

Controlled inhalation, retention (Kumbhaka), and exhalation phases guided by subtle visual cues.

04 / ANALYSIS

Spectral & Coherence Findings

Power spectral density (PSD) decomposition and cross-frequency coupling across breathing cycles.

Theta-Alpha Synchronization

Marked increase in fronto-midline theta power and sustained alpha synchronization during extended retention intervals.

Cardio-Respiratory Coupling

Phase locking between low-frequency respiratory rhythms and vagally mediated HRV power bands.
