Welcome to this digital gallery space. Each room exhibits a single painting alongside a brief curatorial statement detailing its form, texture, and inspiration. Scroll down to enter the exhibition rooms.

GALLERY ROOM 01

Chromatic Study I

Oil on canvas 120 × 150 cm 2024

This piece investigates the subtle transition between deep darkness and emerging light across thick impasto layers. The bold strokes reflect an emotional tension captured during late-night studio sessions in early winter. Through varying directional brushwork, the canvas changes dynamically as ambient light shifts across its surface.

GALLERY ROOM 02

Velvet Shadow

Acrylic and oil pastel 100 × 100 cm 2025

An exploration of fluid harmony, where diluted pigment creates ethereal background washes contrasted with sharp graphic marks. The composition balances chaotic texture with quiet, negative space to evoke a feeling of meditation. It serves as a visual inquiry into how memory fades and reforms over time.

GALLERY ROOM 03

Echoes in Ochre

Mixed media on linen 90 × 140 cm 2025

Drawing inspiration from natural erosion, earth pigments and raw charcoal dust were rubbed directly onto raw unprimed linen. The vertical emphasis creates an architectural presence that commands quiet contemplation from the observer. The tactile surface highlights raw material honesty over illusionistic rendering.

GALLERY ROOM 04

Luminous Field

Oil glazing on wood panel 150 × 180 cm 2025

Layered over six months using traditional translucent oil glazing techniques, this canvas produces an internal luminescence. The painting captures the precise moment light breaks through dense morning fog over water. Its expansive scale invites the viewer to step close and submerge themselves in subtle color fields.

GALLERY ROOM 05

Fragment VIII

Collage and cold wax 80 × 80 cm 2026

Part of an ongoing series dealing with architectural ruin and urban decay, built with cold wax medium and torn paper fragments. The surface was repeatedly painted, scraped away, and rebuilt to mirror natural weathering processes. It questions what remains when structural forms collapse under environmental weight.

GALLERY ROOM 06

Nocturne Study

Charcoal and oil on canvas 110 × 130 cm 2026

A stark monochrome composition focusing on spatial silence and deep tonal contrast. Dark charcoal sweeps intersect wet white oil paint to create soft, atmospheric smoke effects. The piece serves as a quiet meditation on isolation, movement, and darkness within urban nightscapes.
